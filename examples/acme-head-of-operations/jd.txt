Head of Operations
Acme Corp | Payments division | London (hybrid)

About Acme Corp
Acme Corp is a leading payments processor serving more than 40,000 merchants across the UK and Europe. Our payments division is growing quickly and needs an operations leader to scale with it.

The role
Reporting to the Chief Operating Officer, you will own end-to-end operations for merchant onboarding, settlement, disputes and customer support, leading a team of around 200.

Requirements
- 10+ years of operations leadership, ideally in payments or financial services
- 5+ years of hands-on experience with Salesforce in an operations setting
- Experience with ServiceNow for incident, change and workflow management
- Working knowledge of Snowflake for operational analytics
- PMP certification (required)
- Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or higher (desirable)
- Track record of measurable improvements in cost, speed and quality

AI in operations
- You will identify and deploy practical AI and automation in operations, from dispute handling to fraud triage, and report measurable outcomes
- Experience introducing AI tools to teams and building colleague adoption is expected
